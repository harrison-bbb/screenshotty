Launch plan
Can you turn my notes into a launch plan for the Northwind dashboard? We ship on the 14th.

Here's a plan built around a two-week runway. It front-loads the things that take longest (assets and early access) and keeps launch day itself simple.

1. Two weeks out: get the story straight

  • Write a one-line pitch and test it on five customers.
  • List the three screenshots that explain the product best.
  • Pick one metric to lead with: time saved per weekly report.
  • Draft the changelog entry and the launch email.

2. Ten days out: build the assets

Capture clean screenshots of the overview, the revenue chart and the customer table. Keep the same background and frame on all of them so the launch reads as one piece.

3. One week out: early access

Invite your twenty most active customers. Ask each for one sentence you can quote, and fix anything that confuses more than two of them.

Send invites on a Tuesday morning. Replies come back fastest early in the week.

4. Launch day

  1. 9:00: publish the changelog and product page.
  2. 9:15: send the email to all customers.
  3. 9:30: post on X and LinkedIn with the screenshots.
  4. All day: answer every reply within an hour.

5. Track it

Add a small snippet so you can see sign-ups from each channel:

track("launch_signup", {
  source: utm.source ?? "direct",
  plan: account.plan,
  referrer: document.referrer
})

6. The week after

  • Share the numbers with the team on Friday.
  • Turn the three best customer quotes into a short post.
  • Write down what you'd change for the next launch while it's fresh.
Perfect. Can you make the launch email shorter?

Here's a version that fits on one phone screen:

Subject: Your weekly report, in 30 seconds

The new Northwind dashboard puts revenue,
churn and your top customers on one page.
No more stitching reports together on Monday.

It's live in your account now.
→ Open your dashboard

It keeps the one promise (less time on reports), one proof point and one button. Anything longer gets skimmed.

Checklist

  • Screenshots captured and styled
  • Early access feedback in
  • Email scheduled for 9:15
  • Posts drafted for X and LinkedIn
  • Someone on replies all day

Good luck with the launch. Send me the numbers afterwards and I'll help you write the follow-up.
