Installing takes a minute

Screenshotty is free, so it isn't signed through Apple's paid developer program. macOS asks you to confirm it once; after that it opens like any other app.

Drag it into Applications

Open the downloaded Screenshotty.dmg and drag the icon onto the Applications folder.

Open it, then click Done

Everything a screenshot needs

Press ⌘⇧2, click what you want, and it's already styled.

Smart selection

The selection locks onto the paragraph, panel, button or window under your pointer, including sections of web pages.

Never leak a secret

Emails, API keys, tokens, card numbers and IPs are found on your Mac and hidden in one click, or automatically.

Fixes sloppy captures

Stray slivers and cut-off content are trimmed, margins evened out, and the result centred to the pixel.

Mockups and frames

Safari-style browser windows, a MacBook or an iPhone with their real screen shapes, light or dark.

Styles for every network

One-click looks with exact sizes for X, LinkedIn, Instagram, launches and docs. Save and share your own.

Built for teams

Brand kit watermark, shared team style folders and re-editable project files, all free.

Smart backgrounds

Gradients, mesh wallpapers, your own images, or a background generated from your screenshot's colours.

Annotations

Arrows, boxes, text, numbered steps, blur and redact that stay attached to your content.

Fast export

⌘C to copy, ⌘S to save, or drag the preview straight into Slack, X, Figma or Finder.
