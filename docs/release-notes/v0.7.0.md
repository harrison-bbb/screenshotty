## Easier to install, and it updates itself

- **Automatic updates.** Screenshotty now checks for new versions. When one is out you get a notification and an
  "Update to Screenshotty …" item in the menu bar menu; one click shows what's new and installs it. There's also
  **Check for Updates…** in the menu, and a switch in Settings. Updates are signed, so your Mac only accepts
  genuine releases, and Screen Recording permission carries over.
- **A website with a Download button.** [harrison-bbb.github.io/screenshotty](https://harrison-bbb.github.io/screenshotty/)
  has the download, the one-time "Open Anyway" steps, and the Terminal and Homebrew options.
- **A nicer DMG.** Opening the download shows a drag-to-Applications window with the first-open steps right there.

If you're on 0.6.1 or older, install this version by hand once (download, or the one-line installer).
From here on, updates come to you.
