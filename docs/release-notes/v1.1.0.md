## Screenshotty 1.1

Thanks to everyone in the Early AI-dopters community for the ideas in this release.

### Scrolling capture
- Choose **Capture Scrolling Area** from the menu bar, pick the area, then scroll down. Screenshotty joins the page
  into one tall image as you go: long web pages, AI chats, docs and Slack threads.
- Sticky headers and footers appear once, not on every screen, and the scroll bar is left out.
- **Auto-scroll** scrolls for you and stops at the bottom (needs the Accessibility permission).

### Copy text
- **Capture Text** copies the words in any area you pick, instead of a picture.
- In the editor, **Copy Text** (⇧⌘C) copies the text from the screenshot. It's read on your Mac; nothing is uploaded.

### Pin to screen
- Keep a screenshot floating above every window while you work. Drag to move it, scroll or pinch to resize,
  double-click or press Esc to close, and right-click to copy, save, edit or fade it.
- Pin from the editor, the floating preview (right-click), or choose **Pin it on screen** under After capturing.

### Your own hidden words
- Settings → **Your hidden words**: add client names, people or internal domains, and Screenshotty hides them
  along with emails, API keys and card numbers, including with Auto-hide.
- Command line: `--hide "Acme Corp, Jane Doe"`.
