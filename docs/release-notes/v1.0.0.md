## Screenshotty 1.0

Beautiful, perfectly centred screenshots for your Mac. Free and open source.

### New: it keeps itself up to date
- When a new version is out you get a notification and an **Update to Screenshotty …** item in the menu bar menu.
  One click shows what's new and installs it. There's also **Check for Updates…** and a switch in Settings.
- Updates are signed, so your Mac only accepts genuine releases, and Screen Recording permission carries over.

### Easier to install
- A website with a Download button: [harrison-bbb.github.io/screenshotty](https://harrison-bbb.github.io/screenshotty/).
- Opening the download shows a drag-to-Applications window with the one-time "Open Anyway" steps.

### Fixes
- Turning **Auto-hide** off now uncovers sensitive info again, and there's a **Show** button once it's hidden.
- Arrows, boxes and labels land exactly where you draw them, even when the export is enlarged.
- Typing a new label no longer restyles the previous one, and a deleted label stays deleted.
- Keyboard shortcuts keep working after typing in the address or watermark field.
- The smart picker works properly across several displays.
- Phone photos (JPEG/HEIC) are no longer sideways, and vivid Display P3 colours stay vivid.
- The iPhone mockup's Dynamic Island never covers content in landscape.
- A mostly blank capture is no longer shrunk to a single mark.
- Shared style files can't freeze the app with extreme values.
- Instant copy and save no longer pause the app; batch export keeps files that share a name.
- Smaller fixes to shortcuts, launch at login, the menu bar menu and permission prompts.

If you're on 0.6.1 or older, install this version by hand once (download, or the one-line installer).
From here on, updates come to you.
